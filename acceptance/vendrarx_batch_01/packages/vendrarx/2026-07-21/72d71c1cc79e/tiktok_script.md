BEATS  
1. Most NAD+ research comes from animal studies — translation to humans is unclear.  
2. Oral precursors like NMN and NR have more human data than injectable NAD+.  
3. Even then, the human trials are small and short-term. No large controlled studies yet.  
4. Injectable NAD+ has almost no published human data — just anecdotes and clinic reports.  
5. Bottom line: we don’t have rigorous evidence that injections outperform precursors.
