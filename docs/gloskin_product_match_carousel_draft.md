# Product match carousel — copy and visual draft

Status: complete five-slide copy draft; visual production pending updated release screens. This is the first priority for the next content batch, not a rendered or ready-to-post asset. No generation jobs have been submitted.

## Concept

Show people that GloSkin can help them check how a product fits their skin profile. Lead with the products they already own. This introduces the newly shipped product-match feature instead of stretching the older face-scan creative to cover it.

Primary cover: **Check how your products match your skin.**

Cover subline: **See your product match score in GloSkin.**

Optional future hook to test in a separate creative: “You checked the reviews. Have you checked your skin match?” Do not render an extra variant yet.
