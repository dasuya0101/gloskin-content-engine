# GloSkin screen-video recording pack

Four ready-to-record scripts for the September 18–24 plan. These are voiceover and editing scripts, not claims that the recordings already exist. Record the current release; match the actual UI labels during editing. All voiceover below is brand narration, so it can be recorded by the founder or used as text captions without pretending to be a customer testimonial.

## One capture session can supply videos and carousel screens

Record vertically at the phone's native resolution, ideally 1080p or higher and 30 fps. Use a demo profile without personal details and silence notifications. Record clean app audio or mute it; voiceover can be added separately. Avoid music during capture so the footage is reusable.

For each flow, record 30–60 seconds of raw footage. Leave about three seconds still before and after each useful screen, tap deliberately, and hold result screens long enough to read. Capture the actual flow rather than trying to hit the final edit's timestamps while using the app.

Also save native screenshots of the profile, product lookup, product result, result explanation, Today/routine and Glo Score screens. Screenshots make sharper carousel source assets than frames from a compressed video. Use the same demo profile and product across the product-match captures.

Editing target: 1080×1920 video. Keep added captions away from the bottom controls and right-side platform buttons; verify the final platform preview. Use readable short subtitles and simple cuts. If a wait is removed, mark the transition “Wait trimmed” rather than implying instant processing. Never animate a fabricated score or move the app UI to imply an interaction that did not happen.

## Additional bases for the remaining video concepts

### Talking head — Start with your shelf (15–20 seconds)

Script: “You don't need a new skincare haul to try product match. Start with something already on your shelf. Check its match score in GloSkin, based on your skin profile, and read the explanation. It's a guide—not a promise that a product will work.”

Shots: 0–4 s, speaker holding a product; 4–13 s, cut to the real product/result capture; 13–20 s, speaker or GloSkin end card. Use this as brand narration. If the speaker is generated, label it as an AI-generated spokesperson; do not frame the speech as a real customer outcome. Product and screenshot must refer to the same item.

### Animated product-match explainer (15 seconds)

Script: “Your skin profile. A product you want to check. A match score in GloSkin. Read the details, then use them as a guide. Check the match on your shelf.”

Storyboard: 0–3 s, skin-profile card; 3–6 s, product card; 6–10 s, reveal the unchanged real result screen; 10–13 s, emphasize the existing explanation; 13–15 s, GloSkin CTA. Animate the surrounding cards and transitions, not the score or in-app UI. Current match screenshots are needed before assembling it. This is a production base, not a new completed animated ad.
